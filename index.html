ABOUT

Inspired by people,
not trends.

Groteska was born in Barcelona from the beauty of the ordinary.
Old cafés.
Plastic chairs.
Summer afternoons.
Grandparents.
Local football.
Loud conversations.
Sun-faded signs.

Created by designer Claudia Tardito in Barcelona, we make heavyweight graphic T-shirts with the same philosophy: timeless, ironic and made to age well.

MANIFESTO

We don't design for everyone.

Groteska celebrates everyday icons. The grandmother hanging laundry. The old man arguing about football. Plastic chairs outside a neighbourhood café. Sun-bleached shop signs. Bad souvenirs. Good stories.

We believe clothing should carry personality long before it carries a logo. Every illustration starts as an observation from real life, transformed into something worth wearing, collecting, and talking about.

Our humour is irreverent. Our production isn't.

That's why every T-shirt is made to order by our trusted production partner, using premium natural cotton, high-quality digital printing, and responsible manufacturing standards. Producing only what is needed means less waste, better quality, and a more thoughtful way of making things.

No hype. No overproduction. No trend chasing.

Just timeless graphics, honest craftsmanship, and the belief that making people smile shouldn't come at the expense of the people who make our clothes—or the planet they make them on.

ABOUT

Inspired by people, not trends.

Groteska was born from the beauty of the ordinary. Old cafés. Plastic chairs. Summer afternoons. Grandparents. Local football. Loud conversations. Sun-faded signs.

We create heavyweight graphic t-shirts with the same philosophy: timeless, ironic and made to age well.

MANIFESTO

We don't design for everyone.

Groteska celebrates everyday icons. The grandmother hanging laundry. The old man arguing about football. Plastic chairs outside a neighbourhood café. Sun-bleached shop signs. Bad souvenirs. Good stories.

We believe clothing should carry personality long before it carries a logo. Every illustration starts as an observation from real life, transformed into a graphic that feels collectible rather than seasonal.

Heavyweight cotton. Slow production. Timeless humour. Designed to become better with age.
